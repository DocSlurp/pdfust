# Independent content-alignment review

The reviewed version is `p003-content-alignment-v2` (catalog commit `8dffa7f`). All 18 focused tests passed. An independent 3,000-case bounded random comparison found no difference in matched/substituted counts when reference and prediction were swapped; missing/extra counts and character denominators swapped as required. This verifies the tested invariants, not exhaustiveness over arbitrary tables.

The v1 review found a directional tie defect. For reference `[["BETA", "Alpha Beta"], ["20", "Alpha 20"]]` and prediction `[["1", "BETA"], ["ALPHA", ""], ["Alpha 20", "20 Alpha"]]`, v1 produced 12 matched characters forward and 10 in reverse. V2 selects a deterministic normalized-matrix orientation before scoring, then transposes caller-role counts and witnesses. The corrected fixture has 10 matches and four substitutions in either direction, with missing/extra counts and denominators exchanged.

Content recovery deliberately differs from schema acceptance. Inserting a blank column can preserve all characters and produce perfect content precision/recall while recording a blank-cell gap. Joining adjacent cells can also recover content with a synthetic separator. Those cases must not receive accepted-schema or complete-table credit solely from this metric. The exact schema companion remains separately versioned; existing strict cell scores and targets remain unchanged.

The review confirmed monotone row alignment, prefix/suffix retention, penalties for missing and extra characters, and failure of a reordered-row fixture. Case, punctuation and internal numeric whitespace remain significant. Original merged spans are validated before normalized orientation; unsupported spans or resource budgets produce unavailable diagnostics, not invented zero scores. The bounded two-pass alignment is a heuristic rather than a globally optimal joint row/column edit solver.

Aggregate reports must preserve unmatched tables, negative false positives, operational failures and all intended page denominators. Per-table alignment on matched successful tables alone cannot establish whole-run content quality. Selected partial crops require their explicit scoring scope; historical fallback output must not become model decision credit. No model input, frozen output, reference or primary score was modified during this review.
