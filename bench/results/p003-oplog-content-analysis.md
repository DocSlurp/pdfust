## Annotation policy

The historical nine-case slot denominator is explained by the author-unresolved tenth case, `invoice-context-04` (index 14). Its gold choice is `unresolved`, authored records are empty, and original slot exactness is `None`. All nine determinate cases have explicit `rNcM` body slots, including authored empty value slots. There is no additional missing slot among the determinate targets. This audit excludes the unresolved case from content scoring while retaining it in each ten-session denominator and in original grouping/stop metrics. An actually missing slot would receive its own explicit exclusion; predictions never supply target coordinates or strings.

Prediction text joins immutable source atoms in each cell’s declared source-ID order. Matrices retain declared rows, columns and internal blank rows, trimming only trailing unused prediction capacity. References come from the original selected A/B source-owned assembly, preserve explicit empty slots and validate exact body source conservation. Unassigned and deferred words remain omissions. Content precision/recall describe emitted bodies for the nine known cases, including unfinished sessions; completed recovery additionally requires the original `finished` ending. An abstention can retain content without becoming successful completed extraction.

## Source-visible next work

The original wires retain 42 distinct before-state hashes across 83 calls and 1,687,747 request bytes. Menus contain 120 assign, 186 leave-unresolved, 83 finish, 106 move, 20 join and three split options across those repeated calls. These are menu entries, not unique states or independent opportunities. Chosen operations comprise 60 assign, ten finish, three join, two split and eight abstentions. Source conservation can therefore coexist with outstanding topology edits.

A useful CPU-only derivative is a predeclared termination replay over every original trace: evaluate whether stopping at a fully assigned/no-deferred source state avoids a later abstention or loop, while retaining every emitted matrix and measuring the risk of stopping before an available join, split or move. Such a policy must be frozen from source state and menu availability, include all 20 sessions, and avoid target-based stopping rules. Any avoided original requests would be counterfactual exposure, not realized API savings; original costs stay intact. This report does not launch that derivative.
