# wild-v1 page transcription: hosted, open-GPU and native models

DEV-only comparison on the 32-document wild-v1 corpus (24 born-digital
templates plus 8 photographed/scan-like documents, 12 languages and scripts).
Each page is rendered to a JPEG on a white background and transcribed to
Markdown. Scoring:

- **Text F1** is the bigram F1 against the template truth.
- **Exact tables** counts tables whose cell matrices match exactly.
- **Cell recall** is the mean fraction of truth cells found.
- **Image-text recall** measures text drawn only inside raster images.

`bench.wild_vision.evaluate` scores every arm the same way. It strips Markdown
and HTML markup, olmOCR front matter, and DeepSeek-OCR grounding tags before
text scoring. Failed and abstained pages stay in the denominator.

Reproduce:

- Rebuild these tables with `python -m bench.wild_models_report`.
- Rerun arms with `python -m bench.wild_vision --run-hosted --models M [--reasoning-effort E]` for hosted models.
- Use `python -m bench.open_ocr_remote --model M` behind a vLLM tunnel for open models.
- Per-page records and complete outputs stay in the ignored
  `bench/results/pdf-master-artifacts/wild-v1/vision-*/` directories.
- Every arm is synced to Langfuse as `wild-v1 vision <arm>` (vision-v1) or
  `wild-v1 <directory> <arm>` (raster and open directories).

## Notes

- **Reasoning effort.** New hosted models are first evaluated at their lowest
  accepted reasoning effort. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash accepts `none`. GLM-5.3 Flash
  rejected both `none` and `enabled: false` with HTTP 400 on every request; its
  lowest accepted level is `minimal`. The rejected `effort-none` GLM records
  are retained under `admission-refused-20261012/`, and are not model results.
  - A default-effort GLM arm was stopped after 22 of 96 pages, because it
    averaged several times the latency of the other arms. It is retained under
    `vision-v1-stopped/`.
  - A complete default-effort DeepSeek V4.1 arm is retained as its own row.
  - One default-effort GLM page timed out waiting for a local transport slot
    before transmission.
- **DeepSeek-OCR-2.** The reported arm uses the published
  `<|grounding|>Convert the document to markdown.` prompt, a 6,144-token
  completion cap within the model's 8,192-token context, and
  `skip_special_tokens: false`. Grounding tags are stripped before scoring.
  - Its tokenizer registers `<tr>`/`<td>` as special tokens. vLLM's default
    detokenization therefore removed every table-cell boundary. That 320-page
    arm (text F1 0.784, 0/270 exact tables) is retained under
    `vision-open-v1-superseded/` as a serving error, not a model result.
  - An earlier attempt left `max_tokens` at 8,192, so vLLM rejected every
    request with HTTP 400. It is retained under `vision-open-v1-failed/`.
  - vLLM 0.31 also needed `TRITON_ALLOW_NON_CONSTEXPR_GLOBALS=1` to start.
  - With correct serving, DeepSeek-OCR-2 is competitive on upright pages
    (0.87–0.91 text F1). It fails on 90°-rotated scans (0.251). It also
    produces fewer exact table matrices than the other open models. In the
    inspected sample, multi-row headers were split across the wrong rows.
  - Its configuration is not tuned beyond the published prompt.
- **Default vs lowest reasoning.** At default reasoning, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash
  completed 74 of 96 three-level pages with text F1 0.688. It cost
  $0.0064/page with a 30.8 s median latency. At effort `none` it completed
  94/96 with F1 0.901, at $0.0011/page and 12.7 s.
- **Latency.** Median request seconds are per-request client latency, not
  throughput. Hosted arms ran with up to eight ledger transport slots shared
  across concurrent arms. Open models ran 32 concurrent requests against one
  GPU, at 1.2–1.6 pages/s.
- **Cost.** Hosted cost is the sum of per-page ledger charges (provider-reported
  where positive). Open models cost only GPU rental:
