## What the existing new metrics change

The completed 32-run replay preserves every original strict and secondary witness. On natural16 acquisition, Luna1024 has 51.54% content recall versus 20.21% at 640, while strict-or-equivalent complete tables remain four in both. That suggests an acquisition/coverage difference, rather than treating every low strict cell count as a semantic transcription failure. This is one retained run at each setting, not a resolution guarantee.

On random40, Haiku640 recovers content for all 36 expected tables versus 33 strict complete tables, but the three differing span topologies remain incorrect as schema, and its 108-unit negative-page extra table lowers precision. Luna640 has aggregate content precision/recall of 100%, but an extra blank row/altered span prevents one table from being content-complete. Neither observation permits full-structure credit from recovered text alone. The frozen confirmation48 results likewise remain an audit of existing outputs, not a source of new prompt labels.

The next useful comparison is therefore selected-only content precision and full-cohort recall for the three decision families above. It can distinguish abstention/coverage loss, real literal loss and schema disagreement without crediting a retained fallback. Candidate and ownership models must remain separate; header hypotheses reuse generated body strings and cannot be called source-conserved correction.
