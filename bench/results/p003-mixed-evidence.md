## Measurement limits and provenance

All arms are single runs. Stochastic calls, crop choices, admission decisions and provider routing can vary; resolution or encoding differences cannot establish causation without predeclared paired repeats. Runtime transport concurrency is three, while local processing stays serial per run. Concurrent arms share host and provider capacity, so latency and CPU figures are contended observations rather than isolated speedups. Parent process CPU uses process_time; RSS is sampled by psutil every 100ms. Parent RSS peaks are not summed across runs; detector-worker resource scopes remain in the underlying verified full-pipeline evaluations. Hosted GPU use is unmeasured.

Only explicit provider-reported cache read/write counters are observations. Missing counters remain unknown; retained transcript usage is historical and no local replay/cache-hit benefit is inferred from cost or latency. Financial overlays never alter original quality or reports. Full raw evidence and source paths stay in ignored artifacts.
