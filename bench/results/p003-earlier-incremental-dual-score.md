# Earlier decision screens: retained-output dual-score replay

All 48 declared runs retain the full natural DEV denominator: 16 pages, 53 reference tables, 2,333 anchor cells and four negative pages. The replay covers 14 early candidate selectors, 15 boundary repairs, nine structure-admission arms and ten geometry-diagram selectors. These are repeated observations over the same pages, not 768 independent documents.

Every historical legacy strict total and page witness reproduced exactly before secondary scoring. Legacy candidate and boundary outputs include retained baseline tables; their new primary views remove that credit. Source/output inventories and the new attribution plan were frozen and independently approved before references were opened. No inference, retries, output changes or new reference selection occurred.

Candidate quality credits actually selected crops only; abstention, duplicate-only menus and atomic failures contribute zero tables. Boundary quality credits only complete tables changed by applied model joins in successful compositions. Their acquisition, literal text and protected geometry remain historical inputs, so this is conditional changed-table quality rather than cell-only model attribution. A completed response below the join threshold receives no credit. All zero-applied arms remain in the table.

Admission quality includes only admitted source-owned tables. Diagram quality retains independently selected disjoint complete crops on operationally incomplete pages; unresolved/failed crops receive zero credit, and geometry conflicts reject the positive composition. Operational errors are shown separately from primary predicted-table quality.

Content P/R measures alignment units, including synthetic spaces introduced by adjacent-cell joins. Content-perfect does not imply correct rows, columns or spans. Equivalent-table counts use the unchanged conservative schema companion, and strict scores remain separate.

## Interpretation

The early PPLX512 selector has 981 selected-only strict cells, five strict complete tables and six strict-or-equivalent tables, with 95.13% content precision and 61.22% recall. The text arm has 359 selected-only cells rather than its legacy 784 retained-output cells. This makes the admission/abstention effect visible without crediting baseline fallback.

The two applied Clef Omni boundary arms have 42 and 30 strict cells in changed complete-table states, respectively, and zero complete reference tables. Those numbers are conditional changed-table content, not improvements over the untouched baseline. Native response completion alone did not produce applied edits in the other 13 arms.

Admission and diagram counts use their separate evidence contracts. The outline128 PPLX diagram arm has 908 strict cells but only one strict complete table; the content metric does not turn that into complete extraction. Operational failures remain visible even where independently selected partial crops contribute quality.

## Scope and measurements

Rates above reproduce the pinned original operational receipts: candidate processed pages, boundary attempted pages, admission intended pages, and diagram questions. Historical candidate attempted-page fields erroneously contain zero even when questions and selected outputs exist; those raw values remain preserved in JSON and are not published as measured zero performance. They are not fresh complete-pipeline throughput or decision-complete pages. Costs are the independently retained all-attempt API totals; unknown totals remain unknown. Historical generation cost is excluded and was not repurchased.

The boundary inventory contains only four changed region states and five applied joins across two Clef Omni arms. The other 13 arms have no applied changed-table output. This distinguishes successful native responses from successful changes. All models and treatments are shown separately; no fallback model or per-page winner is credited.

The older mixed-evidence queue contains 35 additional candidate arms omitted from this bounded wave. Gate-only and probability-only screens need their task-specific metrics; source-frozen candidate oracles are not achieved model quality. These omissions are explicit rather than counted as rescored.

The complete replay measured 62.593 seconds wall and 62.420 seconds CPU, with 1,269,133,312 bytes peak process RSS sampled every 100 ms. This includes admission, loading and all 48 CPU scorers in one process; no GPU or subprocess inference was used.
