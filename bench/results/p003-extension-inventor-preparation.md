## Mechanism and boundaries

The ownership ledger retains every extracted word's opaque ID, exact literal text and source geometry. Candidate tables own those IDs; values are reconstructed, never authored by a decision model. Repeated equal values therefore remain distinct. Candidate IDs are opaque digest-derived labels sorted independently of policy order. Menu generation reads actual source PDF text, native/text table geometry and vector rules, never generator cell rectangles or target files.

Native/text candidates are supplemented with adjacent row ownership swaps, release-to-prose alternatives, restoration of observed unowned source words, and the empty-table alternative. Every candidate passes complete-source ownership and span-tiling validation. Candidate admission counts separately disclose omitted alternatives. The menu is bounded at 24 states. Full-source admission is 23–168 words per page; stored menus range from 7,528 to 313,241 bytes. Model prompts should project disputes and explicitly count omitted unchanged context rather than send every full-state dictionary.

On **every native-empty source**, without consulting family labels, physical and rolling row proposals plus an intentionally loose two-track midpoint proposal permit false-positive table admission. All eight negatives now have two to four choices. All eight borderless pages have four choices. All other pages have 14–24 choices. A negative page's empty choice is therefore a decision, not a sole-candidate no-op.

The source packet may contain source words, coordinates, raw rules, source render and blind alternatives. Family names, target values, construction receipts, perturbation labels and oracle coverage results must remain outside the model packet. Native baseline identity should also stay outside ordinary selection prompts; explicit stress experiments may expose their perturbed current state. `abstain` retains baseline and is not model success.

## Separately labeled synthetic repair stress

Source-only selection of owned cells produced 48 missing-table-value treatments, 48 doubled-source-ownership treatments, 48 swapped-cell-value treatments and 14 physical wrapped-continuation shifts. They are frozen separately in `stress/{id}.json`, never confused with actual valid extractor output. Missing values move into the unresolved source inventory. Doubled ownership is deliberately invalid and must fail the standard validator. Swapped and continuation-shift treatments preserve source words exactly. Correct alternatives are source geometry states, not reference-derived winners.

These are synthetic repair challenges, not evidence that a production extractor has the same failure rate. Detector/candidate coverage failures must remain failures. The eight borderless sources have no native vector table; text/loose proposals provide alternatives. Exact authored-target availability in the menu remains unmeasured until independent scoring after source/menu freeze.

Keep strict, v2 and wrap metrics alongside omitted/doubled ownership and value-placement diagnostics. Wrap conservation can reward a collapsed all-words state and does not establish correct tables, roles, wrapping or Markdown readability. Independent later scoring and visual review should assess actual improved output.
