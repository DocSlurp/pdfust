## Hypotheses and sequence

1. Source-owned local alternatives can repair orphan values, neighboring-cell
   placement and wrap boundaries without transcription errors. Build 64 pages
   across eight layout families with randomized values, neutral titles, truly
   unnamed headers and prose negatives. Freeze source-only menus before targets
   are opened. Missing correct alternatives are coverage failures.
2. Broad model comparison may reveal useful specialists hidden by whole-page
   extraction scores. Freeze an intended arm/page manifest. Run one compatibility
   page then seven more pages per model; stop only the affected arm on transport
   failure, preserving its complete intended denominator. No inline retries or
   other-model fallback. Public catalog verification precedes paid requests.
3. Localized difference evidence may beat full candidate descriptions, especially
   on small-context models. Compare matched menus with full/difference packets,
   counts of omitted source IDs, and complete local source validation.
4. Image resolution, encoding and prompt framing may change placement quality.
   Explore 128/256/400/512/768/1024 pixel long edges; grayscale and reduced bit
   depth are separately named treatments. Blank-image controls retain the same
   text, options and dimensions. Do not infer resolution effects from different
   model, page or protocol mixes.
5. Combine useful geometry proposals with model admission or local edits, using
   the same model within each experiment. Evaluate real improvement over the
   untouched baseline, including unchanged and negative controls. Preserve
   moderately promising arms for derivatives instead of pruning on tiny samples.
